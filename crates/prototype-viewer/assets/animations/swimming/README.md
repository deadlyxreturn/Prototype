# Swimming clips

Six swimming clips (forward and backward strokes, floating at the surface and
under water, diving), kept as their original BVH files so swimming can be
previewed offline. When they load, the root movement is removed, the loops are
closed and the backward stroke is turned to travel head first. Nothing here is
uploaded automatically: the AO picks swimming animations from your inventory.

`cannonball.anim` is a separate 2.8-second jump-in clip for offline water
previews only.
