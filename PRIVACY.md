# Prototype privacy policy

Prototype is a free, open-source Second Life viewer made by Ryan Johnson.
This page says what it does with your data. Short version: Prototype does
not collect anything about you for itself.

## What Prototype sends, and to whom

- **Second Life (Linden Lab).** When you log in, Prototype sends your user
  name, password and the usual viewer details (version, operating system,
  graphics card) to the Second Life login servers, like every viewer does.
  Everything you do in Second Life goes to Linden Lab's servers. Their
  privacy policy covers it: <https://lindenlab.com/privacy>.
- **Update and news check (GitHub).** At start-up Prototype reads the latest
  release and the news posts of this repository from GitHub, to tell you
  when a new version is out. GitHub sees your IP address like for any web
  page; nothing else is sent.
- **Bug reports (optional).** If you choose to report a bug from the viewer,
  you sign in to GitHub yourself and the report becomes a public issue in
  this repository. Nothing is sent unless you press send, and you see the
  report first.

## What stays on your computer

Settings, chat logs, the cache and saved logins stay on your own PC.
Saved passwords go to your system's credential store (Windows Credential
Manager), never to us. Uninstalling Prototype and deleting its settings
folder removes them.

## No tracking

No analytics, no advertising, no telemetry, no selling of data. Ryan
Johnson runs no server that receives data from the viewer.
